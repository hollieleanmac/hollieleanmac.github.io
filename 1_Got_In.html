Got in

happenings from 2021-2022 remembered 2026/08/17

“She won't get into that school. You have to be someone to go there.” Said my father's now ex-girlfriend to my sick and disabled grandmother behind my back.

Well fuck you bitch!!! I did get in!

First try too, unlike your daughter's friend who applied three years in a row before she got in. Getting accepted to university at 22 tasted oh so much sweeter because she said that. I could honestly thank her for it. I had never felt so thrilled, so accomplished, so rewarded for my efforts in life. And a good chunk of that euphoria was petty spite.

…Only now, after four years of being locked inside the Canadian fine art sphere, do I understand what she meant, and I am inclined to agree with her.

I had no business being at NSCAD University, or any form of academia.
